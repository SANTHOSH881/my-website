How to calculate CGPA

CGPA is calculated by taking the credit-weighted average of your semester SGPAs. This means semesters with more credits have a greater effect on the final CGPA.

Formula: CGPA = Σ(SGPA × Semester Credits) ÷ Total Credits

Example

If one semester has an SGPA of 7.5 with 20 credits and another has an SGPA of 8.0 with 20 credits, the combined CGPA is 7.75.

What is CGPA?

CGPA stands for Cumulative Grade Point Average. It represents your overall academic performance across multiple semesters.
